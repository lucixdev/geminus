Third-party components shipped with GEMINUS
===========================================

GEMINUS is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later
(see LICENSE). This file lists software written by others that is shipped
together with it, and where to find its licence and its source code.

1. Rust crates compiled into the program
----------------------------------------
The exact list, with versions, is src-tauri/Cargo.lock. Every crate compiled
into the released binaries is distributed under a permissive licence — MIT,
Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Zlib, 0BSD, Unlicense, CC0-1.0,
the Unicode licence — or under MPL-2.0. All of them are available from
<https://crates.io>.

2. System libraries bundled in the Linux AppImage
-------------------------------------------------
The AppImage carries a copy of the GTK and WebKitGTK stack together with its
dependencies, taken unmodified from Debian 13 (trixie). Most of them are
covered by the GNU Lesser General Public License; the rest by permissive
licences. The licence of every bundled library is shipped inside the AppImage,
under usr/share/doc/third-party-licenses/.

The corresponding unmodified sources can be obtained for the same Debian
release from <https://sources.debian.org/>, and on request from the address in
the copyright notice.

The .deb and the .rpm bundle nothing: they use the libraries already installed
on the system.

3. Windows
----------
On Windows the interface is rendered by the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime,
which is part of the operating system, is not free software and is not covered
by the licence of GEMINUS; the installer obtains it from Microsoft. See
LICENSE-EXCEPTION.txt.
